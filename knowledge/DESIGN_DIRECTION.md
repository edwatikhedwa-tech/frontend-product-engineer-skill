# Design Direction

For CREATE and systemic REDESIGN, write a compact direction before substantial implementation:

- product personality and emotional target;
- primary user and high-value job;
- visual hierarchy and reading order;
- density target for the task (calm, balanced, or information-rich);
- typography roles and line-length rule;
- surface, border, radius, elevation, and color principles;
- responsive strategy and key breakpoints;
- state and motion principles;
- two to five references with reasoning, not screenshots to copy;
- explicit do/don’t examples.

Use tokens and component principles to make the direction executable. A design direction is not a moodboard and not a substitute for product decisions.

# Design Review / Visual Critic

Review the rendered product as an independent senior reviewer. First inspect the product job and evidence; then separate engineering/UX defects from design-quality judgment.

## Product quality

Ask:

- Can a first-time user tell what this screen is for within seconds?
- Is the primary action obvious without making every action loud?
- Does grouping express the user’s mental model rather than the DOM structure?
- Is information density appropriate for the job and viewport?
- Are typography, spacing, controls, and surfaces coherent?
- Do content changes, errors, empty states, and permissions preserve hierarchy?
- Are interactions discoverable, reversible where appropriate, and keyboard reachable?
- Does the layout survive realistic and unusually long content?

## Visual Critic questions

Ask explicitly:

1. What makes this product visually recognizable?
2. What would a senior product designer criticize immediately?
3. What feels generic or template-derived?
4. Where does the interface feel like an AI-generated SaaS dashboard?
5. Which visual decisions feel accidental?
6. Which visual decisions feel intentional and have product/user/task rationale?
7. Is the product aesthetically coherent across primary, non-happy-path, and narrow states?
8. Does the design support the product’s actual job?
9. Is there unnecessary decoration, novelty, or component-library default styling?
10. Is this the best interface the product could reasonably have within the agreed constraints?

## Evidence and disposition

Record findings with evidence, severity, location, impact, and a concrete fix direction. Record the identity paragraph, rationale for signature decisions, reference synthesis, and anti-generic result. For REDESIGN, link the transformation matrix.

Use qualitative labels WEAK, ACCEPTABLE, STRONG, or EXCEPTIONAL only as a compact summary. Any WEAK or ACCEPTABLE result on visual identity, hierarchy, composition, typography, distinctiveness, or intentionality requires iteration. Do not defend implementation history or replace evidence with a score.

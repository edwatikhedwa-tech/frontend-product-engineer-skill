# Design Review

Review the rendered product as an independent senior reviewer. Ask:

- Can a first-time user tell what this screen is for within seconds?
- Is the primary action obvious without making every action loud?
- Does grouping express the user’s mental model rather than the DOM structure?
- Is information density appropriate for the job and viewport?
- Are typography, spacing, controls, and surfaces coherent?
- Do content changes, errors, empty states, and permissions preserve hierarchy?
- Are interactions discoverable, reversible where appropriate, and keyboard reachable?
- Does the layout survive realistic and unusually long content?
- What would a demanding product owner reject after seeing the real browser output?

Record findings with evidence, severity, location, impact, and a concrete fix direction. Do not defend implementation history.

# Reference Research

Search by the product or UX problem, not by vague style terms such as “beautiful dashboard”, “modern SaaS”, or “premium UI”. Prefer queries such as CRM company list, customer directory, supplier search results, bulk actions table, filter builder, split view, settings navigation, company profile, or data-heavy dashboard.

Use several levels when useful:

1. Product reference — how the whole workflow is organized.
2. Screen reference — how a comparable screen prioritizes information.
3. Pattern reference — reusable table, filter, navigation, or split-view structure.
4. Interaction reference — behavior of search, selection, dialogs, and feedback.
5. Visual-language reference — typography, spacing, density, surfaces, and tone.

For every selected reference record:

- What problem does it solve?
- Why does the hierarchy work?
- Why does the spacing and density work?
- What interaction is reusable?
- What must not be copied?
- How should it be adapted to the current product, users, content, and constraints?

References are benchmarks and reasoning sources. Do not pixel-copy, reproduce proprietary assets, or let a paid reference service become a hard dependency. If Refero, SaaSFrame, or another source is unavailable, use official design guidance, public product documentation, browser inspection, and a clear reasoning log.

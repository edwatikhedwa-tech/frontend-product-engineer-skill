# Spacing and Layout

Define a rhythm before styling details. Use a small spacing scale, consistent control heights, predictable container widths, and a grid that reflects the content.

Check:

- page insets at desktop, tablet, and mobile;
- relationships between title, toolbar, content, and detail areas;
- alignment of labels, fields, table columns, and actions;
- density in data-heavy views versus reading views;
- intentional scroll regions and sticky elements;
- behavior when content grows or disappears.

Do not add borders, cards, shadows, or nested containers merely to separate regions. Spacing and alignment often communicate structure more clearly.

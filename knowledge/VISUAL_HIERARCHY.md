# Visual Hierarchy

- Establish one dominant entry point per view.
- Use size and weight for semantic priority, contrast for emphasis, and whitespace for grouping.
- Keep primary, secondary, and tertiary actions visibly distinct.
- Avoid making labels, metadata, badges, and controls equally prominent.
- Use alignment lines and repeated edges to make scanning predictable.
- Keep headings specific enough to orient the user; do not use oversized text as decoration.
- Review the screen at a glance, at normal reading distance, and while scanning for a known item.

Operational products usually need fast scanability and stable landmarks. Marketing pages can be more expressive, but the same hierarchy rules apply.

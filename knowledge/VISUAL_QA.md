# Visual QA

Run the real app and inspect screenshots at the viewport matrix. Review:

- hierarchy and typography;
- spacing, rhythm, alignment, and density;
- component consistency and visual noise;
- states and feedback;
- responsive behavior and intermediate widths;
- long content, missing values, and localization expansion;
- clipping, overflow, overlap, and unexpected scroll;
- focus, contrast, semantic structure, and keyboard behavior;
- perceived polish versus decorative complexity.

Keep an evidence log with viewport, URL/state, screenshot path, findings, fixes, and re-check result. The final screenshot is an acceptance artifact, not a formality.

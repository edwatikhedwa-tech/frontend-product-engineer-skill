# Release checklist

- [ ] Scope and mode are explicit.
- [ ] Design Director direction and design rationale exist before substantial CREATE/REDESIGN implementation.
- [ ] Reference categories were selected deliberately and synthesized into a product-specific system.
- [ ] No unrelated files or business behavior changed.
- [ ] Project checks pass at the appropriate level.
- [ ] Real app starts using the documented command.
- [ ] Browser evidence exists for important flows and viewports.
- [ ] Stress content and long-content behavior are checked.
- [ ] Overflow/clipping and intentional scroll regions are understood.
- [ ] Accessibility evidence exists and manual gaps are stated.
- [ ] Independent review happened after implementation.
- [ ] Anti-generic gate passed; identity and distinctiveness are supported by rendered evidence.
- [ ] Transformation gate passed for REDESIGN, or missing BEFORE evidence is explicitly recorded.
- [ ] Engineering/UX and Visual results are reported separately.
- [ ] P0/P1 are zero and P2 are resolved or documented.
- [ ] Regression check covers neighboring flows.
- [ ] Handoff states what was verified, not verified, and why.

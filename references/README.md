# References

References are research inputs, not assets to copy. Keep notes about the product problem, hierarchy, density, interaction, what not to copy, and adaptation. Prefer small text analyses and links over large screenshot collections.

Suggested folders when evidence grows:

- `product-patterns/` — whole-product workflows and information architecture.
- `screen-patterns/` — lists, tables, profiles, settings, dashboards, split views.
- `interaction-patterns/` — search, filtering, selection, dialogs, navigation, feedback.

Do not store private project screenshots, credentials, cookies, or copyrighted assets without permission.
